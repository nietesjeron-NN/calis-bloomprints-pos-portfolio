# Practical Phased Roadmap

## Phase 1: Working Offline POS

- Room database with products, sale headers, sale lines, and printer settings.
- Seed catalog for bouquets, flowers, gifts, printing, and custom orders.
- Product browsing, cart quantity controls, customer info, notes, cash checkout, change calculation.
- Sales history and receipt reprint.
- Simple stock adjustment for tracked inventory.
- Bluetooth paired-printer selection, saved width, test print, and receipt print.

## Phase 2: Shop Operations

- Product editor for name, price, category, and stock tracking.
- Discounts, service fees, deposits, partial payments, and custom item pricing.
- Daily sales summary with cash drawer totals.
- Low-stock alerts and stock movement history.
- Receipt branding options and optional footer messages.

## Phase 3: Reliability And Reports

- Export sales to CSV.
- Backup and restore local database.
- Refunds, voids, and manager PIN.
- Z-reading style daily close.
- Printer diagnostics for code page, line width, paper cut, and drawer kick.

## Phase 4: Growth

- Optional cloud sync across devices.
- Customer order calendar and pickup reminders.
- Delivery tracking and rider notes.
- Loyalty/customer history.
- Role-based staff accounts.
